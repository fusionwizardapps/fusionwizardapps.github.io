Water Wobble

Inside the game

Simple to start, hard to put down

Grids grow from cosy 6×6 boards to sprawling 10×10 mazes, and the water gets faster as you go.

100 levels

The valve and the tank land on different edges every time, with rocks to route around and less time to plan as you climb.

Pipeline Survival

An endless valley that scrolls as you build and speeds up the longer you last. How deep can you go?

Arrives in the first update

Daily challenges

A fresh puzzle every day and a gift waiting each time you come back.

Power-ups

Undo a placement, slow the flow, break a stray pipe or skip a piece you can't use.

Make it yours

Unlock pipe skins and water colours in Wobble's Shop. Some are earned free with the stars you collect.

Meet Wobble

A very expressive drop of water who cheers, teases and drops tips as you play.

Fair and friendly

  • Free to play, with no account and no sign-up.
  • Ads are optional rewards you choose to watch, plus the occasional break between levels. A one-time purchase removes ads for good.
  • Your progress stays on your phone and the game works offline.
