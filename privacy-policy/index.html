Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fusion Wizard Apps ("we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile games, including Water Wobble (the "Apps"). We are an independent developer. Our Apps do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details.

1. Information handled by the Apps

Game data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, coin balance, unlocked items, and daily-streak status are stored only on your device. Android may include this data in your Google account's automatic device backup, under your control in Android Settings > Google > Backup. You can erase all game data at any time with "Reset Progress" in the game's Settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our Apps show ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to limit how often you see the same ad, the AdMob SDK collects your device's advertising ID, IP address (which indicates your approximate region), device and operating-system information, and basic interaction data such as ad views and clicks. It also collects crash and diagnostic information. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google's list of ad technology partners is at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903. We do not receive personal information from AdMob; we see aggregated reports only.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs, the starter bundle, and "Remove ads" are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and holds your payment details; the App receives only a confirmation that a purchase was made so it can unlock the item. Purchases are tied to your Google Play account and can be restored on reinstall.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They do not send any information to us or to anyone else, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android notification settings.

2. Your choices

Ads and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings ("Privacy options"). Anywhere in the world, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation there.

Deleting your data

All game data lives on your device: use "Reset Progress" or uninstall the App. Because we hold no account or personal data about you, there is nothing further for us to delete. For data processed by Google (advertising, purchases), use the controls in your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com.

Legal rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any right or ask a question, email us at the address below.

3. Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used our Apps in a way that provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

4. Security and data transfer

The information described above is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and processed by Google on servers that may be located outside your country, under Google's safeguards. We store no personal data on servers of our own.

5. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. Continued use of the Apps after a change means you accept the updated policy.
