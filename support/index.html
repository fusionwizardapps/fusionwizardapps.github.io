Help & support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about Water Wobble. For anything else, or for our other apps, email us and you'll hear back from the developer directly.

I bought something. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Purchases belong to your Google Play account. Sign in to the same Google account on the new phone, install the game, then open Settings → Privacy & ads → Restore purchases. "Remove ads" and the starter bundle come back straight away.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy & ads → Privacy & ads settings. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, this reopens the consent message so you can change your answer at any time. Anywhere, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Levels, stars, coins and unlocked items are stored by the game on the device, so it works offline. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes that progress; purchases can always be restored as above.

How do I start again from level 1?

Open Settings → Progress → Reset progress and confirm. This clears your levels, stars, coins and unlocked items on this phone. Purchases are not affected.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and we don't collect your name, email address or contacts.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Please email us. Tell us the app, your phone model and what happened; a screenshot helps a lot.
